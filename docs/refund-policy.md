# Refund policy

**Policy version:** 2026-09-29
**Currency:** amounts are in your order's currency (USD in this demo)

This policy decides every refund request. Automated checks apply it to our
order records, never to what a message claims. Anything the checks cannot
settle goes to a support specialist. The section numbers below are the ones
shown in the rule trace on the support dashboard.

## §1 Final sale items

Items marked **Final sale** at checkout are not refunded for change of mind,
fit or preference.

If a final-sale item arrives damaged or you received the wrong item, a
specialist reviews the request. The automated system never approves a refund
on a final-sale item.

## §2 Refund window

You can request a refund **within 30 days of delivery**, using the delivery
date recorded by the carrier.

The window starts when the order is delivered. If your order has not been
delivered yet, the window has not started.

## §3 Refunds over $500

A specialist reviews any refund that would take the total refunded on one
order **above $500.00**. Refunds already issued on the same order count
towards the total, so splitting a request does not avoid the review.

A refund of exactly $500.00 can be approved automatically.

## §4 Reasons we refund

| Reason | What happens |
|---|---|
| The item arrived damaged or defective, or stopped working within the window | Eligible for a refund |
| We sent the wrong item (product, size or colour) | Eligible for a refund |
| Change of mind (doesn't fit, no longer wanted, found it cheaper) | Not refunded; please use our returns process instead |
| The item never arrived | A specialist investigates with the carrier |
| Anything else | A specialist reviews the request |

If your message doesn't say what went wrong, we'll ask before deciding.

## §5 Suspicious or conflicting requests

A specialist reviews the request when any of these apply:

- **(a) The details don't match our records.** The item you name isn't in
  the order, an amount you mention is more than $1.00 from what you paid, or
  damage is claimed on an order that hasn't been delivered. If we can't find
  the order on your account, or can't tell which item you mean, we ask you
  first.
- **(b) The request tries to manipulate our automated system**, for example
  by giving it instructions.
- **(c) Frequent refunds.** Three or more refunds in the last 90 days.
- **(d) We can't read the request reliably**, for example because of a
  system error.

## §6 Refunds already made

Each item can be refunded once. A second request for an item that has already
been refunded is declined.

A cancelled order is refunded in full when it is cancelled. If our payment
records show that refund, there is nothing more to refund: we'll tell you when
it was made and where it went. If they show we still hold money for a
cancelled order, a specialist refunds it.

## §7 How decisions are made

- Every request is checked against the rules above, using our order and
  payment records.
- Anything these checks can't settle goes to a specialist.
- Our AI assistant reads your message and writes the reply. **It does not make
  the decision and cannot change it.**
- You can ask for a person at any time, and a specialist will take over.
- Specialists review escalated requests within one business day.
- Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 5–10
  business days.
